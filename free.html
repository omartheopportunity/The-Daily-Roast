Questions

Before you sign up

Is New Faces, New Places really free?

Yes. No card, no catch. It's how we meet people before anyone commits to anything.

When is it, and how long?

Thursdays at 12:00 PM EST, for 45 minutes, on Zoom. Your link comes by email and text after you save your seat.

Do I need my camera on?

Yes, please. It's a conversation room, and faces are what make the connections stick.

Can I meet people in my own city?

That's what the "Who do you want to meet?" question is for. Pick "People near me" and tell us where you're based, and we'll put locals in your breakouts when we can.

What is the Super Saver discount?

A discount on your first order of The Daily Roast, sent to your email after you sign up.
